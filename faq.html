SIBT V TOOL — Frequently Asked Questions

Is SIBT V TOOL free?

Yes. It is a free mobile video maker, and no signup is needed.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. It works in your phone's browser. Just open the tool, add your pictures and audios, and make your videos.

How are pictures and audios paired?

Automatically, in file-name order (1, 2, 3…). To control the order, name your files in the order you want them to appear before you add them.

What quality should I choose?

720p is the fastest option. 1080p gives sharper quality but takes longer to make.

What does the repeat setting do?

It repeats the audio in a section. Each section shows its final duration, so you can see how long the finished video will be before you create it.

What is the spectrum option?

It draws animated, moving music bars along the picture while the music plays. It takes extra time, so turn it on only when you want that effect.

Why is my video taking a long time?

Videos are made on your own device, not on a server, so the time depends on your phone, the length of the audio, and the quality you chose. Long audios and 1080p videos take longer. Keep the screen open while the video is being created.

Can I make many videos at once?

Yes. You can add many pictures and audios, create all sections at once, and download them all.
