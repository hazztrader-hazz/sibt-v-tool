How to Use SIBT V TOOL

SIBT V TOOL is a free mobile video maker. It turns your photos and audios into HD videos, right on your phone. No signup needed.

Step 1 — Add pictures
Tap Add pictures and select many at once.

Step 2 — Add audios
Tap Add audios and select many at once.

Step 3 — Check the pairing
Pictures and audios are paired automatically in file-name order (1, 2, 3…). Name your files in the order you want them to appear.

Step 4 — Choose quality and repeat
Pick a quality: 720p is fastest, 1080p is sharper. Choose how many times each audio repeats. Each section shows its final duration, so you know how long the finished video will be.

Step 5 — Add spectrum (optional)
Turn on Add spectrum to draw moving music bars on the picture. This takes extra time.

Step 6 — Create and download
Create your videos, then download them. You can create all sections at once and download them all.

Tips for best results

  • Keep this screen open while videos are being created.
  • For the fastest result, use 720p. Use 1080p when you need the sharpest quality and have more time.
  • Long audios and 1080p videos take longer — that is normal, because the video is made on your own device.
